Frequently asked questions

What is custom business software?

Custom business software is built around the way your business actually works, instead of asking your team to adapt to a generic tool. Examples include customer portals, booking systems, reporting dashboards, and tools that replace spreadsheets or connect systems you already use.

When does a small business need custom software instead of an off-the-shelf tool?

Off-the-shelf tools are often the right starting point. Custom software tends to make sense when your process is specific to your business, when you are working around a tool with spreadsheets and manual steps, or when you need several systems to share data. A consultation is a good way to find out which applies to you before committing to a build.

Can you replace our spreadsheets with proper software?

Yes. Replacing spreadsheet-based processes with a purpose-built system is one of the most common reasons businesses ask us about custom software. We start by understanding how the spreadsheet is used today, then scope a system that covers those workflows.

How does a project start?

We start with a consultation to understand the problem and your goals, then agree a clear scope and proposal, and only then begin building. This keeps the project focused on outcomes you have agreed.

How much does custom business software cost?

Cost depends on what the software needs to do, how many users and systems are involved, and how much needs to be integrated. We do not quote a figure without understanding the scope, which is why the consultation and proposal come first.

Not sure what you need yet?

Start with a consultation. We will help you work out whether custom software is the right move and what it would involve, before you commit to a build.
